YASH ALL-STAR BIRTHDAY EXPERIENCE — FOOTBALL + CRICKET

OPEN
1. Extract Yash_Birthday_AllStar_Experience.zip.
2. Open the project folder.
3. Double-click index.html in Chrome or Edge.

EXPERIENCE FLOW
1. First two sections use a Wembley Stadium football background.
2. Tap HIT FOR THE GOAL to score a goal; a celebration starts and the site moves to cricket.
3. The later sections use a Narendra Modi Stadium cricket-ground background.
4. Play the batting mini-game: tap BOWL THE BALL, then tap SWING THE BAT when prompted (or press Space).
5. A successful six blows out the 11 candles.
6. Tap CUT THE BIRTHDAY CAKE.
7. Type a birthday wish and send it to the stars.

PERSONAL MEMORIES
All eight uploaded photos, individual descriptions, hidden messages, birthday letter and wish cards are retained.
